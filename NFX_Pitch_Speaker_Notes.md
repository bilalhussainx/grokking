## Anticipated Questions & Answers

### Q: How is this different from ChatGPT voice?
**A:** "ChatGPT is general-purpose. We specialize in structured learning with curriculum, progress tracking, and pedagogical AI tuning. Plus our multi-language support is purpose-built for education, not bolted on."

### Q: What about content quality?
**A:** "Sixty-nine plus courses with over two thousand lessons already built. We mix original content with expert partnerships. Every lesson is AI-enhanced, not AI-generated from scratch—there's human curation and structure."

### Q: Why would users pay when ChatGPT is free?
**A:** "Same reason people pay for MasterClass over YouTube—curation, structure, and experience. We save users time by providing a guided learning path with integrated voice practice. Twelve dollars a month is nothing compared to the time saved not prompting a general AI."

### Q: What's your moat?
**A:** "Four things: voice AI integration depth—ElevenLabs with emotion-based tones; content library scale—two thousand plus lessons; user progress data for personalization; and community brand in underserved niches where we have no competition."

### Q: How do you acquire users?
**A:** "Start with niche communities—Reddit's learnprogramming has four million members, languagelearning has two million. These are self-selecting, high-intent users. Product Hunt for tech credibility. Then expand through content marketing and partnerships with coding bootcamps."

### Q: What about mobile?
**A:** "Fully responsive web app works on mobile today. Native iOS and Android apps are in the roadmap for Series A—it's one of our stated milestones. The web app already supports voice on mobile browsers."

### Q: Have you considered B2B?
**A:** "Yes—enterprise and campus licenses are one of our growth levers. Imagine every student at a coding bootcamp getting Samsara access as part of their tuition. Or high schools using it for AP Computer Science prep. Huge TAM expansion."

### Q: What's the tech stack?
**A:** "Next.js frontend, WebSocket for real-time voice, ElevenLabs for text-to-speech, Web Speech API for speech-to-text. Scalable, modern, proven technologies. Hosted on Vercel for global edge deployment."
