## 1. UK / UCAS (2027 entry)
- Equal-consideration deadline: 13 Jan 2027 18:00 UK (official: https://www.ucas.com/events/2027-entry-deadline-for-all-undergraduate-courses-except-those-with-a-15-october-deadline-475546). Same page and https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/dates-and-deadlines-for-uni-applications say 15 Oct 2026 (18:00 in 2025 wording) for Oxford, Cambridge and medicine/dentistry/vet courses, 30 Jun 2027 last date before Clearing, 23 Sep 2027 final date. Late applications may be considered if vacancies remain.
- Applications can be submitted from 12 May 2026 for 2027 entry once references complete (official, dates-and-deadlines page). Search summary also gave 1 Sept 2026 as first day completed applications are sent to universities: UNVERIFIED on the page I read.
- Extra opens 25 Feb 2027 (official: https://www.ucas.com/events/2027-entry-extra-opens-477516). Last day to add Extra 1 Jul and Clearing opens 2 Jul 2027 (secondary: UCAS search-result snippet, key-dates page not fetched).
- Reply deadlines (official: https://www.ucas.com/applying/after-you-apply/types-of-offers/replying-to-your-offers): last decision by 31 Mar 2027 -> reply 5 May; by 12 May -> 2 Jun; by 14 Jul -> 21 Jul 2027; no reply swaps after 22 Jul 2027; one firm + one insurance.
- Fee: GBP 34.50 for 2027 cycle, up to 5 choices (6 for conservatoires) (official: https://www.ucas.com/faqs/what-is-the-application-fee-for-the-2027-cycle). Fee waivers for e.g. care leavers, free-school-meals (official: filling-in page below).
- Choices: max 5; max 4 in any one of medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine, veterinary science; usually only one of Oxford or Cambridge, exceptions for some graduate medicine routes; reference required from someone not family/friend/partner (official: https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/filling-in-your-ucas-application). Predicted grades: that page does not address them; UNVERIFIED.
- Personal statement 2026 entry onwards (official: https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/writing-your-personal-statement/how-to-write-your-personal-statement-for-2026-entry-onwards): three questions: (1) "Why do you want to study this course or subject?" (2) "How have your qualifications and studies helped you to prepare for this course or subject?" (3) "What else have you done to prepare outside of education, and why are these experiences useful?" Min 350 characters per answer, 4,000 characters total including spaces.
- Admissions tests:
  - UCAT 2026 (official: https://www.ucat.ac.uk/about-ucat/ucat-test-dates/): registration opened 20 May, booking 23 Jun, testing 13 Jul-24 Sep; booking deadline 16 Sep 2026 15:00 UK (now closed); results to universities early Nov. 2027 dates not yet published.
  - LNAT (official: https://lnat.ac.uk/registration/dates-and-deadlines/): testing from 1 Sep 2026; Oxford/Cambridge: register/book by 15 Sep, sit and submit UCAS by 15 Oct 2026 (Oxford Foundation Year to 13 Jan 2027); KCL/LSE/UCL sit by 31 Dec 2026; others register by 20 Jan 2027, sit by 25 Jan 2027; Bristol/Durham all 13 Jan 2027; international late applicants test to 31 Jul 2027.
  - ESAT / TMUA / TARA: registration opened 1 Jun, booking 20 Jul, closes 28 Sep 2026 18:00 UK, tests 12-16 Oct 2026, results 16 Nov; second sitting booking opens 26 Oct, closes 21 Dec 2026, tests 4-8 Jan 2027. Confidence: secondary (tutelaprep/yourdreamschool search summaries); official UAT-UK page not reachable (DNS failed). Also Oxford-specific tests (PAT, MAT, etc.) NOT researched.

## 2. Canada
- OUAC Undergraduate: "Undergraduate Application Deadline (Group A): January 15, 2027" (official: OUAC Guidance Dialogues Resource Guide Fall 2026, https://guidance.ouac.on.ca/docs/resource_guide.pdf, read via saved PDF text). Group A = current Ontario high school applicants (old 101); Group B = all others (old 105); rename for 2026-27 (secondary: search summary citing guidance.ouac.on.ca / ouinfo.ca announcement; those pages returned 404/403). Search summary: base fee $159 for first 3 choices, Group B has no OUAC deadline (set by each university), doc evaluation fees $25-$200 (secondary; ouac.on.ca returned 403): UNVERIFIED.
- Supplementals (official, same Resource Guide): Waterloo AIF required for all Engineering (excl. Architecture), all Mathematics programs and Computing and Financial Management; online interview required for Engineering. Queen's: Commerce, Nursing, Health Sciences supplementary application due 15 Feb 2027. McMaster: 11 direct-entry programs need a supplementary application (Arts & Science, Technology, Computer Science, Engineering I, Health Sciences, iBioMed, Integrated Business & Humanities, Integrated Science, Midwifery, Nursing I, Wilson College); Business optional. UofT: some specialized programs, see https://future.utoronto.ca/supplemental-applications; UTM Computer Science (TMX) supplemental $52, Theatre audition $50. U of T Architecture supplemental due 7 Feb 2027 (campus attribution not checked). Exact McMaster/Queen's due dates beyond the above: UNVERIFIED.
- EducationPlannerBC: application platform for 25 public BC institutions (official: https://www.educationplannerbc.ca/). Fees/deadlines not on page read: UNVERIFIED.
- ApplyAlberta: online application and transcript transfer for Alberta's 26 publicly funded institutions, operated by APAS; Alberta.ca account required; site noted access delays on 2 Oct 2026 (official: https://prod.applyalberta.ca/). Fees/deadlines: UNVERIFIED.
- McGill (official: https://www.mcgill.ca/undergraduate-admissions/apply): applications via future.mcgill.ca/apply starting 1 Oct; fee CAD 140.16 non-refundable; second program in same application free; some programs need CASPer/letters. Fall 2027 deadlines depend on educational background: UNVERIFIED. CEGEP context for out-of-province students: NOT researched.
- Grade conversion for US students: UofT US page (https://future.utoronto.ca/apply/requirements/international-high-school-secondary/usa/us-admissions/) via search snippet only: Grade 12 graduation, test-optional policy stated for fall 2025 (not confirmed for fall 2027), Engineering SAT sections 650 / ACT 30 (28 Math & Science). Confidence: secondary/UNVERIFIED for 2027.
- OSAP (official: https://www.ontario.ca/page/osap-ontario-student-assistance-program): 2025-26 and 2026-27 applications open; first-time full-time applicants complete an interactive information module (~15 min). Eligibility (secondary, search summary of ontario.ca): Ontario resident and citizen/PR/protected person; 60% course load; program starting 1 Aug 2026-31 Jul 2027; international students not eligible; grants and loans.

## 3. US systems
- Common App 2026-27 first-year: season launch; blog says 31 Jul 2026 (official: https://www.commonapp.org/blog/common-app-opens-application-launch-2026-27-season/), search snippet said Saturday 1 Aug (conflict, see below). Prompts unchanged, 7 prompts, 250-650 words (secondary: collegeessayguy.com for prompts 5-6 text and 650 limit, plus search summaries of commonapp.org; Common App's prompts page body did not render for me). Prompt 5: "Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization that sparked a period of personal growth and a new understanding of yourself or others." Prompt 6: "Describe a topic, idea, or concept you find so engaging that it makes you lose all track of time. Why does it captivate you? What or who do you turn to when you want to learn more?" Prompts 1-4 and 7 as in search summary (secondary). Fee waiver eligibility (FRPL, SAT/ACT waiver, USDA income guidelines, Pell): secondary search summary; commonapp support page did not load. Some colleges do not accept the Common App waiver.
- Coalition/Scoir application: NOT researched (UNVERIFIED whether still active).
- UC (official: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/how-to-apply/applying-as-a-first-year/index.html): fall 2027 start working 1 Aug; filing period 1 Oct-30 Nov (NOT Dec 1). Fee/waiver on that page: not stated, UNVERIFIED.
- UC PIQs (official: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/how-to-apply/applying-as-a-first-year/personal-insight-questions.html): answer 4 of 8, 350 words max each, equal weight. Topics: leadership; creativity; greatest talent; educational opportunity/barrier; significant challenge; academic subject that inspires you; community/school improvement; anything else.
- UC TAG (official: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/admission-requirements/transfer-requirements/uc-transfer-programs/transfer-admission-guarantee-tag.html): six campuses (Davis, Irvine, Merced, Riverside, Santa Barbara, Santa Cruz; not Berkeley/UCLA/San Diego); fall: TAG 1-30 Sep, UC application 1 Oct-30 Nov; winter/spring: TAG 1-31 May, UC app 1-30 Jul; submit via UC TAP; must meet GPA/coursework in TAG agreement; missing TAG deadline still allows regular transfer application. Unit rules (30 semester/45 quarter UC-transferable units, one UC-E English and one UC-M Math by fall 2026, max 80 semester/120 quarter units) are secondary (search summary of UC pages); GPA and excluded majors are in the TAG matrix, not read.
- FAFSA 2027-28 (official: https://financialaidtoolkit.ed.gov/tk/announcement-detail.jsp?id=fafsa-updates): form now available, covers 1 Jul 2027-30 Jun 2028. Exact launch date 23 Sep 2026 is secondary (news summaries); ed.gov press release returned 403.
- CSS Profile (official: https://cssprofile.collegeboard.org/): 2027-28 sign-in available; "free for families who make up to $100,000 a year"; used by 2,300+ colleges and programs. Per-application fee not found (fee page 404): UNVERIFIED.
