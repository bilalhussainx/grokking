**Q: How is this different from ChatGPT voice?**
A: ChatGPT is general-purpose. We specialize in *structured learning* with curriculum, progress tracking, and pedagogical AI tuning. Plus, our multi-language support is purpose-built for education.

**Q: What about content quality?**
A: 69+ courses with 2,284+ lessons already built. We mix original content with expert partnerships. Every lesson is AI-enhanced, not AI-generated from scratch.

**Q: Why would users pay when ChatGPT is free?**
A: Same reason people pay for MasterClass over YouTube—curation, structure, and experience. We save users time by providing a guided learning path with integrated voice practice.

**Q: What's the moat?**
A: (1) Voice AI integration depth, (2) Content library scale, (3) User progress data for personalization, (4) Community/brand in underserved niches.
