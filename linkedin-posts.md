## POST 6: Spanish Lesson Snippet (Company Page)

**Visual:** Attach `public/deck-images/talk-desktop.png` (voice conversation interface)

---

Day 1 of Spanish on Samsara.ai. Here's what you actually learn:

Not just "Hola."

Lesson 1 teaches you the greetings system:

Formal (for interviews, meeting elders, business):
- Buenos dias (Good morning)
- Buenas tardes (Good afternoon)
- Buenas noches (Good evening)

Informal (for friends, neighbors, casual settings):
- Que tal? (How's it going?)
- Que pasa? (What's up?)
- Como andas? (How are you doing?)

Then the part textbooks skip -- cultural context:

In Spain, greeting someone you know? Expect two kisses on the cheek. Even between men and women meeting for the first time in social settings.

In Latin America? A handshake in business. A single kiss or hug among friends. It varies by country.

Then you practice. Out loud. With an AI tutor named Sofia.

She greets you. You respond. She corrects your pronunciation in real-time. No judgment. No embarrassment. Just patient practice.

By the end of Lesson 1, you can greet anyone in Spanish -- formally or casually -- and understand the cultural rules that textbooks never mention.

This is what language learning should be.
